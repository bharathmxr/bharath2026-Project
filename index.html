01 — The Place

Built on stilts, not on postcards

Bharath Resort opened in 1998 as a rice-boat repair yard's guesthouse and never quite stopped being one. The same carpenters who fix the kettuvallam hulls built our window frames. Rooms are timber, tin-roofed, and raised a metre above the waterline — because the lagoon rises every monsoon, and we'd rather float politely than fight it.

There is no infinity pool. There is a fourteen-kilometre backwater that does the same job, better, for free.

03 — On the Water

What's included, what isn't

Dawn boat, included

A silent electric boat leaves at 6am for anyone awake enough to catch it. No charge, no booking desk.

Kitchen, not a menu

Meals come from whatever the boats brought in that morning. We'll tell you what it is; you can't pre-order.

No fixed checkout rush

Late checkout is free if the next boat isn't in. Ask at the desk instead of assuming either way.

What we don't have

No pool, no spa menu, no in-room TV. There's a hammock deck and a very good library of water charts instead.
